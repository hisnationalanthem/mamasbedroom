This is a starter commission policy. Edit the wording so it matches exactly how you run your commissions before publishing.

1. General

By commissioning me, the client confirms they have read and agreed to these terms before payment. I reserve the right to decline any commission request for any reason.

2. Age Requirement

Mature-theme commissions are for adult clients only. All characters included in sexual or explicitly mature material must be adults.

3. Payment

Payment method, currency, and whether payment is split or paid in full are confirmed before work begins. No work starts until the agreed payment has been received.

4. Scope

The commission only includes the deliverables discussed in the approved scope. Significant additions or concept changes may require an extra charge.

5. Revisions

Revision allowance is determined up front. Revisions are for refining the agreed concept, not for replacing it with a completely different one.

6. Turnaround

Turnaround estimates are estimates unless a specific deadline is explicitly accepted. Delays caused by missing client information or major changes may extend the timeline.

7. Refunds & Cancellations

Refunds depend on how much work has already been completed. Once substantial work has been delivered, full refunds may not be possible.

8. Client Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate references, preferences, and hard limits.
  • Do not request plagiarism or direct copying of another client’s concept.
  • Communicate changes clearly and in one place where possible.

9. Usage

Unless otherwise agreed, final work is for the client’s personal use on the intended platform. Commercial use, resale, redistribution, or reposting without permission is not allowed.

10. Portfolio Use

I may display finished work in my portfolio unless the client requests privacy before completion.
