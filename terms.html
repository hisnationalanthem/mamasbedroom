Important: This is a practical commission-policy template, not legal advice. Replace any payment, refund, ownership, and turnaround language with the rules you actually intend to use.

1. General

By purchasing a commission, the client confirms that they have read and agreed to these terms before payment. I reserve the right to decline a request for any reason, including requests that fall outside my comfort zone, scope, or platform rules.

2. Age requirement

Clients requesting mature or sexual content must be 18 or older. All characters depicted in sexual or explicitly mature content must be adults.

3. Payment

Payment terms, currency, platform, and whether payment is due in full or in stages will be confirmed before work begins. No work begins until the agreed first payment has been received.

4. Scope

The commission includes only the deliverables listed in the agreed scope. Major additions, concept changes, or new deliverables requested after work begins may require an additional fee.

5. Revisions

Reasonable revisions are allowed only within the revision allowance agreed at the start of the commission. Revisions correct or refine the commissioned concept; they do not automatically include complete rewrites or a new concept.

6. Turnaround

Turnaround estimates are not guarantees unless a specific deadline is explicitly accepted in writing. Delays caused by missing information, late client responses, or major scope changes may extend delivery.

7. Refunds & cancellations

Refund eligibility depends on how much work has already been completed. Once substantial work has been delivered, refunds may be partial or unavailable. If I cancel a commission for reasons unrelated to client conduct, I will communicate what portion, if any, is refundable.

8. Client responsibilities

  • Provide accurate references, preferences, and hard limits.
  • Review drafts or questions within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Do not request copied work, impersonation, plagiarism, or direct duplication of another client’s custom commission.
  • Do not remove credit where credit is required by the agreed usage terms.

9. Usage & ownership

Unless otherwise agreed, the client may use the final commission for personal use and on the platform it was commissioned for. Resale, redistribution, commercial use, training datasets, or claiming the work as self-created is not permitted without separate written permission.

10. Portfolio use

I may display finished work or excerpts in my portfolio unless the client requests privacy before the commission is completed. Private commissions will not be intentionally showcased.

11. Platform rules

The final commission must still comply with the rules of the platform where it is posted or used. I am not responsible for platform moderation decisions, account restrictions, or later policy changes.

12. Communication

Keep commission communication in the agreed channel. Important approvals, scope changes, and payment decisions should be written so both parties have a clear record.
