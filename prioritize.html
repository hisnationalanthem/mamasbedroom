If there is an original upcoming bot of mine that you want sooner, send a priority request. You do not need an official upcoming list: identify the bot by its working title, premise, character, teaser, screenshot, or wherever you saw me mention it.

What this does

It affects my posting order.

A priority request tells me which of my already-planned original bots you most want to see. If I accept it, I can move that bot higher in my personal production/posting queue.

  • Any upcoming original Mama Anthem bot can be named
  • You can identify it even if it only has a working title or teaser
  • I can accept, decline, or apply the priority request
  • You receive a priority request ID after submission
What this does not do

It is not a commission.

The bot remains my original project. Prioritization does not buy control over the concept and does not convert the bot into custom work.

  • No custom scenarios or premise changes
  • No POV, character, relationship, or kink changes
  • No ownership, exclusivity, or private access
  • No guaranteed exact posting date
Priority request ≠ paid commission. If you want a bot built around your own concept or want changes made for you, use the normal commission form instead.
